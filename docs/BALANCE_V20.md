# v0.20 competitive-balance report

This pass addresses three related problems: elite-player impact being diluted by aggregate squads, elite clubs suffering implausibly deep league collapses, and coach quality lacking a clear league-versus-tournament identity.

## Major domestic leagues

Six fixed universes produced 30 editions across La Liga, Premier League, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1.

- Preseason favorite won: **66.7%**
- One of the preseason top three won: **96.7%**
- Average finish of the preseason favorite: **1.77**
- Favorite finishing below fifth: **0 of 30**

The favorite is still not guaranteed to win, but elite rosters should no longer drift to seventh, ninth or eleventh without a genuinely poor team/coach combination.

## Major knockout tournaments

Eight fixed universes produced 24 editions across Champions League, Libertadores and World Cup.

- Champions League top-five favorite champion: **75%**
- Libertadores top-five favorite champion: **100%**
- World Cup top-five favorite champion: **75%**
- Combined top-five favorite champion: **83.3%**

Single-match surprises and occasional dark-horse champions remain possible.

## Superstar in a secondary league

A controlled Molde scenario used one 90-rated Legend striker and one 86-rated Rare teammate across five fixed seeds.

- Club finishes: **1st, 2nd, 2nd, 3rd, 1st**
- Striker scoring ranks: **2nd, 1st, 1st, 1st, 1st**
- League-title rate: **40%**
- Top-three club finish: **100%**
- Top-scorer rate: **80%**

A Haaland-level player in Norway can still lose a title, but should visibly distort that league and usually dominate its scoring race.

## Coach focus

Two otherwise equal Spanish clubs were given identical 90-quality Legend coaches. One coach had Regularity focus and the other Tournament focus.

Across six fixed seeds:

- Regularity coach average league points: **93.67**
- Tournament coach average league points: **89.33**
- Regularity coach finished ahead: **5 of 6**
- Regularity coach Copa del Rey final appearances: **2**
- Tournament coach Copa del Rey final appearances: **4**

The focus is influential without replacing player quality or tactical profile.

## Ballon d'Or podium diversity

Across six fixed universes:

- Three players from the same club: **0**
- Podium containing only two clubs: **1**
- Three different clubs represented: **5**

A double-winning club can still place two players. A treble-level season can occasionally place three, but one trophy no longer automatically fills the whole podium.
