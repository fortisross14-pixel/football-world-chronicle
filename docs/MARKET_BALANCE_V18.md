# Goalkeeper and Elite-Migration Balance — v0.18

The deterministic test covers two complete seasons plus a controlled superstar migration scenario.

## Goalkeeper prevalence

- Goalkeepers represent roughly 19% of the active named-player pool because each visible squad requires one.
- They account for 7.7% of all transfers.
- They account for 12.0% of elite transfers.
- They win 4.4% of open competition MVP awards.

Position-specific Best Goalkeeper awards remain intentionally guaranteed and are excluded from the open-award rate.

## Elite migration

A controlled 25-year-old, 97-rated Generational player was placed at Flamengo after three seasons without reaching or winning the Libertadores final. In the next market he moved to Manchester City for $155.5m.

Elite rookies can still develop outside Europe. Migration pressure rises after age 21 based on rarity, quality, club prestige, contract status and happiness. A recent major continental title sharply reduces that pressure, allowing a Libertadores winner to retain its star longer.

## Market-value correction

Previous rarity multipliers could value a 97-rated outfield player above $350m, making transfers mathematically impossible. v0.18 introduces realistic position- and rarity-sensitive ceilings. Goalkeeper ceilings are materially lower than outfield ceilings.
