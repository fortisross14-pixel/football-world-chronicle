# FHoF peak club eras — v0.19

A club-team Hall of Fame entry is a two- or three-season peak under one uninterrupted coach.

The engine now:

1. Groups completed seasons by club and coach.
2. Splits separate coaching spells when seasons are not consecutive.
3. Evaluates every consecutive two-season and three-season window in each spell.
4. Scores weighted wins, goal difference and trophies by competition relevance.
5. Keeps the strongest window from that coaching spell.

This prevents a long tenure's final three seasons from replacing a clearly stronger earlier peak, such as a three-season Champions League dynasty.
